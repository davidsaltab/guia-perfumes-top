Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Guía Perfumes Top respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using our application and its integration with TikTok.

2. Information we may process

When a user authorizes the application through TikTok, we may process information made available through the TikTok API, such as account identifiers, profile information, authorization tokens, and information required to publish content.

3. How we use information

Information is used only to provide the application's functionality, including connecting authorized TikTok accounts, managing content, and publishing videos through automated workflows.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information is only shared with service providers when necessary to operate the application or when required by law.

5. Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect account credentials, authorization tokens, and other information processed by the application.

6. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Revoking access

Users may revoke the application's access to their TikTok account at any time through their TikTok account settings.

8. User rights

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information by contacting the application operator.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the application operator through the contact information provided in the TikTok application.
